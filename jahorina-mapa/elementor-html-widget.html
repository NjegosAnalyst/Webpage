Mapa staza Jahorine

Ski centar Jahorina ima 25 staza i 14 žičara: 7 plavih, 16 crvenih i 2 crne staze, uz vezne staze između njih. Za noćno skijanje osvijetljene su staze 1, 1a, 1b, 1c, 6, 7, 8, 8b i 9. FIS homologovane staze: 3, 5, 6, 6b, 8 i 8a.

Ski runs of Jahorina

Jahorina ski resort has 25 runs and 14 lifts: 7 blue, 16 red and 2 black runs, connected by link runs. Floodlit for night skiing: runs 1, 1a, 1b, 1c, 6, 7, 8, 8b and 9. FIS homologated runs: 3, 5, 6, 6b, 8 and 8a.

Lifts

  • 1 Poljice – gondola, 1500 m, 4.5 min ride
  • 2 Ogorjelica I – six-seater chairlift, 1330 m, 5 min ride
  • 3 Ogorjelica II – double chairlift, 1050 m, 9 min ride
  • 4 Rajska – six-seater chairlift, 700 m, 2.2 min ride
  • 5 Olimpik – platter lift, 620 m, 6 min ride
  • 6 Skočine – six-seater chairlift, 1310 m, 6 min ride
  • 7 Poljice I – T-bar lift, 560 m, 3 min ride
  • 8 Gondola – gondola, 1100 m, 3 min ride
  • 9 Trnovo – six-seater chairlift, 570 m, 2 min ride
  • 10 Spust – platter lift, 150 m, 1.4 min ride
  • 11 Javor – T-bar lift, 670 m, 3.4 min ride
  • 12 Oštra glava – platter lift, 115 m, 1.3 min ride
  • 13 Prača – T-bar lift, 980 m, 4.4 min ride
  • 14 Trnovo I – T-bar lift, 579 m, 3.4 min ride
